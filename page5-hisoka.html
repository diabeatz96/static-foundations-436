Hisoka Morow is an uncanny Hunter seeking all the fun in the world.

Hisoka became a hunter for more leeway to kill. That is his only reason. He can come across as friend or foe with the previous 4, though most of the time he is minding his own business. He spends his time looking for worthy opponents to fight, and particularly has his eye on the leader of the Phantom Troupe, Chrollo Lucifer. He uses his Nen ability, Bungee Gum and sharp cards to fight in battle. Though a strange person, he is a force to be reckoned with.

Bungee Gum has both the properties of rubber and gum!
