Killua Zoldyck is another young Hunter, born and raised an assassin.

Killua is one of the youngest in a family of assassins, all bent on killing for business. He first went to the Hunter Exam to escape his home circumstances. This was when he met Gon, his first real friend who changed his life perspective. Instead of living as a cold blooded assassin, Killua wishes to do more with his life, forming connections and making memories. However his brother, Illumi, fears Killua slipped out of his control, and will do whatever he can to make sure he remembers his place.

He uses his assassin instinct and ability to aid him in battle, though he also uses a YoYo to fight. He's also learned to control and use electricity!
