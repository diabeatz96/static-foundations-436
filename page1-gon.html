Gon Freecss is a young, cheery Hunter and former fisherman!

Gon became a Hunter to find his father, who so happens to want NOTHING to do with him. He lets nothing stop him from training to become the most powerful, and loathes the thought of being any weaker than his current state. He may seem like an angel on the outside, but on the inside is a sinister, unmoveable force yearning for more power.

His best friend is Killua, and his other friends are Kurapika and Leorio! He would rather stay away from Hisoka, though.
