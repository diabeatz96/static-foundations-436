Kurapika Kurta is the last member of the Kurta Clan, driven by revenge.

Kurapika became a Hunter to make use of its many advantages, resources, and leeway when conducting business. He is out for revenge on the notorious Phantom Troupe, responsible for murdering over 100 Kurta Clan members to obtain their Scarlet Eyes. He can use his chains to bind his opponents hearts to a pact they must obey. He has a soft spot for Gon, Killua, and Leorio, but he is still drowning in an indescribable emptiness.

Kurapika's chain ability, Emperor Time, allows him to use 100% of each Nen category when combined with his Scarlet Eyes, making him truly formidable.
