Leorio Paradinight is a Hunter who wants a normal life in medicine.

Leorio is an aspiring doctor who became a Hunter for the money. He wants to be a doctor to save others as there was a time where he could not save his close friend, who passed away from disease. He so happened to meet Gon, Killua, and Kurapika, making his Hunter journey a more interesting one to say the least. He helps out the three with their needs, but has no grudge or desire to a power monster.

Leorio was the last of the 4 to learn Nen, and he gave Gon's father a much deserved punch!
